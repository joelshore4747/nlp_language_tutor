## Datasets

### IMDb 50K Movie Reviews

The IMDb dataset consists of 50,000 movie reviews labelled for binary sentiment classification (positive or negative). The dataset is balanced, with an equal number of positive and negative samples, making it suitable for supervised learning tasks without requiring additional resampling techniques. It is widely used as a benchmark for evaluating text classification models and was originally introduced by Maas et al. (2011). In this project, the dataset is used to support experimentation with text representation methods such as TF-IDF and to validate classification performance.

**References:**  
Lakshmipathi, N. (2019) *IMDB Dataset of 50K Movie Reviews*. Kaggle. Available at: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/lakshmi25npathi/imdb-dataset-of-50k-movie-reviews (Accessed: 16 April 2026).  
Maas, A.L. et al. (2011) *Learning word vectors for sentiment analysis*. ACL.

### WiLI-2018 Dataset

The WiLI-2018 dataset is a multilingual benchmark designed for written language identification tasks. It contains text samples from 235 languages, primarily sourced from Wikipedia, with each language represented by a balanced number of paragraphs. This dataset enables the evaluation of language detection models across a wide linguistic spectrum. In this project, WiLI-2018 is used to train and evaluate the language identification component of the system, ensuring robustness across multiple languages and reducing the risk of language misclassification.

**References:**  
Thoma, M. (2018) *The WiLI benchmark dataset for written language identification*. arXiv:1801.07779. Available at: https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.07779 (Accessed: 16 April 2026).  
Mexwell (n.d.) *WiLI-2018 Dataset*. Kaggle. Available at: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mexwell/wili-2018 (Accessed: 16 April 2026).
